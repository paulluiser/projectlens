Scope: whole web app (landing in persuade mode; projects, reports, editors in operate mode).
Audience: presenter walking internal management through a month; readers browsing the shared report afterwards.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A monthly review is a surveyed route. Each report is a sheet of a survey map: where the project stood, what ground it covered, where it crossed hard terrain, and the route ahead into next month.
FORM: Ordnance-survey sheet. seed: seed:b838f9dbcc61a51f
WORLD: White map paper, contour sienna hairlines, survey blue for selection and water, woodland green for completion, ochre for carry-over, map red reserved for destructive actions. Workhorse humanist sans with tabular figures; grid references (sheet, month, revision) as quiet metadata.
SIGNATURE: Progress drawn as an elevation profile of the project route: walked ground filled, you-are-here marker at project completion, remaining route dashed toward next month. Landing hero is a live Three.js contour terrain.
SHELL: Familiar sidebar, header search, breadcrumbs, chapter tabs; the world enters only via type, palette, density, and the signature.
RISK: The metaphor must never hide a figure; every drawn element carries a plain label and number.
Raises: from wayfinding signage, presenter controls show only the next decision; from exposure records, quantities always in tabular figures.
